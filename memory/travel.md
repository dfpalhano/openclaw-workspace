## Destination Intel

### Bali
- La Favela (Seminyak) — favourite venue, good mix of Europeans + locals
- Ku De Ta, Potato Head — also on the list
- Good for: working, massage, natural social scene, decompress
- Co-working/villa setup works well — timezone GMT+8 (+2 from Brisbane)
- Best for the "sophisticated fun" opening leg of a trip

### Thailand — Pattaya
- Home base for Thailand leg
- Soi 6: daytime short-time, efficient, no negotiation
- Walking Street: evening atmosphere
- Bangkok weekend mid-trip (Nana Plaza, Soi Cowboy) — quality higher than Pattaya
- Nana Plaza: higher quality, more upscale
- Soi Cowboy: intimate, good vibe
- Avoid: Patpong (tourist trap)
- Pattaya gets monotonous after ~10 days — Bangkok resets the head
- Short time: 1,500–2,500 THB depending on venue

### Philippines — Angeles City
- ABC Hotel: solid base, clean, central on Fields Ave
- Everything walkable from hotel
- Internet: get Smart or Globe SIM backup
- GMT+8, same as Bali — Brisbane mornings free before AU hours
- Friendlier/warmer vibe than Pattaya, less transactional
- Many guys prefer Angeles over Pattaya for this reason
- End-of-trip destination — relaxed landing before home
