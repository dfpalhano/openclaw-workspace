## Slide 8: Risk Assessment & Mitigation

### **Technical Risks:**
- **Data Migration** - Historical data integrity
  - *Mitigation:* Parallel systems, incremental migration
- **API Stability** - 24/7 availability required
  - *Mitigation:* Load testing, monitoring, fallbacks

### **Business Risks:**
- **Staff Adoption** - Change management
  - *Mitigation:* Early involvement, comprehensive training
- **Customer Disruption** - During transition
  - *Mitigation:* Phased rollout, house-by-house migration

### **Financial Risks:**

  - *Mitigation:* Phased funding, contingency reserve
